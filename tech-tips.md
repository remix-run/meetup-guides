## Rehearse the switch from slides to a live demo

Before a meetup with remote viewers, ask another organizer to join the stream on a second device. Switch from the slides to the editor or browser as you will during the talk. Ask the viewer to read one detail aloud, such as the route path or the first line of an error.

If the text is hard to read, zoom in on the editor or browser, share a smaller window, or prepare a labeled screenshot. Repeat the switch once with the same detail.

Use the optional [hybrid pointing rehearsal record](https://light-tracer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/hybrid-pointing-rehearsal-record-open-v1.pdf) (CC BY 4.0 PDF) to note what the remote viewer could read.
